**Description:**

IconCraft is a free Mac app that changes how other apps present themselves — their name and icon — across the Dock, Finder and Launchpad.

It was built for a simple, annoying problem: replacement apps and vibe-coded apps often work like a well-known app, but they sit on your Mac under an unfamiliar name and icon, so you go looking for something that isn't labelled the way you expect. IconCraft fixes the label, not the app.

- Give an app the original app's name and icon (PhotoCraft presenting as Photoshop, for example), or use your own name and icon instead.
- Works system-wide. The app underneath is unchanged and keeps working and updating as before.
- Every change is reversible — restore the original name and icon at any time.
- Built-in detection for ArtCraft apps, and it works on other third-party apps and apps you've made yourself.
- Free and open source (MIT), with no account and no telemetry.
- Signed and notarised DMG. macOS 12.3 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

IconCraft is an independent project and is not affiliated with ArtCraft/Storytold, Adobe, Microsoft, Figma or any other app maker. It doesn't modify or redistribute anyone else's app.

Download it at iconcraft.co, and the source is on GitHub under gshipman223/IconCraft.

**First comment (from Gavin, the maker):**

Hi Product Hunt, I'm Gavin, I made IconCraft.

The honest origin story: I was using apps like PhotoCraft, liked them, and still kept losing them in my own Dock because I was scanning for "Photoshop". After the third or fourth time, I figured it was easier to change the label than to retrain my brain.

So IconCraft gives an app the name and icon you actually look for — the original app's, if that's what you want, or your own. The app itself is untouched underneath, and if you change your mind, one restore puts everything back exactly as it was. No account, no telemetry, it's free and MIT-licensed, and the download is signed and notarised so it opens like any normal Mac app.

It's at v0.13.3, it's a real thing you can download and use today, and it's not finished — which is why I'm here. If you use ArtCraft apps, or you've vibe-coded your own replacements for things, I'd love to know whether this solves the "where did that app go" problem for you, and what it gets wrong.

I'll be here answering questions all day. Thanks for taking a look.
