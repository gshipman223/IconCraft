IconCraft is installed

Next: open IconCraft from Applications and complete Finish setup.

  1. Run the permission checks.
  2. If App Management is blocked, use the button provided to open System Settings, switch IconCraft on, then return and choose Check again.
  3. Decide whether to keep the optional background helper on.
  4. Choose Finish setup.

You can reopen the same screen later from Setup & permissions in IconCraft, or from its menu bar icon. Because macOS may treat an updated, locally signed copy as new, recheck App Management after future updates.

IconCraft is ad-hoc signed, not Developer-ID signed or notarised. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
