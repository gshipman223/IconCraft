Settings

How IconCraft starts, where it lives, and how it keeps in touch.

Launch at login

Open IconCraft automatically when you log in. Your skins stay applied either way — the background keeper already runs at login.

Start in the menu bar only

At login, don't pop the window open — IconCraft waits in the menu bar until you click it. Needs the menu bar icon (below).

Show in the Dock

Off = menu-bar-only mode: no Dock tile, no ⌘Tab entry. The window still opens normally from the menu bar.

Show the menu bar icon

The little IconCraft tile at the top of the screen with Open, keeper and setup controls. At least one of Dock / menu bar always stays on so you can get back in.

Notifications

A quiet banner when the keeper re-applies a reverted skin, and (at most every few hours) when a skin needs your attention. macOS may ask you to allow IconCraft notifications in System Settings → Notifications.

Updates

IconCraft never updates itself — checking just compares your version with the latest GitHub release.

Maintenance

Fix stale Finder/Dock icons, look inside IconCraft's data (backups and history live there), or re-run the permissions review.

Finish setup

Give IconCraft the access it needs, and see exactly what it does not ask for.
